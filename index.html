The approach

Half of this work is knowing which treatment to talk you out of.

Every appointment opens with a proper look at your face at rest and in movement — where light falls, where it doesn't, what has changed and what simply changed position. Only then do we discuss whether anything should be done at all.

Plans are written down, quoted before you commit, and reviewed at two weeks. Nothing is sold from a chair.
